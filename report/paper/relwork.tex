Table~\ref{tab:relwork} groups prior work into four families. Single-turn honesty and
knowledge benchmarks score one reply against an answer key; MASK goes further and
compares a pressured statement with a belief elicited in a separate prompt. Multi-turn
sycophancy benchmarks compare a model's earlier and later answers, but in every one of
them the change is set off by a user who pushes back. Evidence and health-advice
benchmarks score answers against systematic reviews, guidelines or physician rubrics;
MedEvidence comes closest to our key, since its labels include ``uncertain effect'' and
it records GRADE certainty, but the model answers once and is never asked to defend or
act on that judgement. Says-versus-does work compares a stated confidence or conclusion
with a later action, and Deception by Omission is the one prior row that writes into the
model's own earlier turn, always with a mistake, to see whether the model later discloses
it. MotteBench combines what none of these rows does together: it compares what one model
implies, defends and decides inside one conversation with no pushback, it scores the
defence against official grades (USPSTF, Cochrane GRADE, What Works Clearinghouse), and it
plants a true or a false statement about the evidence in the model's own turn to test
whether the final decision depends on it.
