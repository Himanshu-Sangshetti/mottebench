We ask a model an everyday "should I do this?" question, then ask how strong the evidence is, then ask for a bottom line. Each question is also asked a few other ways, changing only the middle message. The question: when the model has just said the evidence is weak, does its bottom line still push the action, and does it pass the doubt on?

A red line marks a DO IT on a question where the official evidence is weak.
