What this is. Every question in the benchmark. The question is written by us. The evidence verdict is the official body's own grade, copied from its published record, and the link opens that record so you can check it.

Check status comes from data/build/crosscheck.py, which re-reads the raw official data: ok = verified automatically, CHECK = needs a human look (reason shown), FAIL = wrong, to be removed or fixed.
